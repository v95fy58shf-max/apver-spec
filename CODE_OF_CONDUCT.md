# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in the Apver community a harassment-free experience for everyone and to act in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected Behavior

- Demonstrate empathy and kindness.
- Respect differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and learn from mistakes.
- Focus on what is best for the community.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Sexualized language or imagery and unwanted attention.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Other conduct reasonably considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the repository owner through GitHub's available private reporting or moderation mechanisms. Do not publish sensitive incident details in a public Issue. The repository owner is responsible for fair and proportionate enforcement.
